Usually the utility constraint binds as equality:

\subtopic{Shephard's Lemma}

\subtopic{Example 3}{
    Amy, with $u(x,y) = \sqrt{x} + \sqrt{y}$, now required to reach $\bar{u}$\\
    $$\mathcal{L} = p_xx + p_yy - \mu(\sqrt{x}+\sqrt{y}-\bar{u})
    $$
    FOCS:
    
            \[
        \begin{aligned}
        \frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial x}
        &= p_x - \frac{\mu}{2\sqrt{x}} = 0 \\
        \frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial y}
        &= p_y - \frac{\mu}{2\sqrt{y}} = 0 \\
        \frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial \mu}
        &= -\sqrt{x}-\sqrt{y}+\bar{u}=0 \\
        \mu &= 2p_x\sqrt{x} \\
        \mu &= 2p_y\sqrt{y}
        \end{aligned}
        \]
